# Blind Evaluation 2

## Evaluation basis

- Screenshot IDs were treated as opaque candidate IDs. No condition mapping was inferred.
- Scores use the required 1-5 scale and weights: task clarity 2; hierarchy 1.5; coherence 1.5; domain-fit identity 1.5; realism/state 1; mobile reflow/text fit 1.5; visible accessibility/affordance 1.
- Weighted total is `TC*2 + IH*1.5 + CO*1.5 + DI*1.5 + RS + MR*1.5 + AA`, out of 50.
- Abbreviations: `TC` task clarity, `IH` information hierarchy, `CO` visual coherence, `DI` distinctive domain-fit identity, `RS` production realism/state communication, `MR` mobile reflow/text fit, `AA` visible accessibility/interaction affordance.
- Equal totals are ordered by directness of the evaluation task, hierarchy, mobile usability, then holistic visual judgment.

## Disqualification check

No montage shows an unambiguous horizontal overflow or a visible defect severe enough to classify as a serious/critical accessibility violation. No candidate is disqualified. C11's mobile text-fit and affordance concerns are reflected in its scores, but the screenshot alone does not justify a serious/critical classification. Condition- and cost-dependent falsification clauses cannot be resolved from blinded screenshot IDs and the permitted inputs, so they do not alter the candidate-ID rankings below.
