## Interpretation

- Evaluator Pearson correlation was `r = 0.571`, so small score differences remain taste-sensitive.
- All B3 outputs averaged `44.875`; the six cherry-picked incumbents averaged `46.0`. This is an intentionally conservative comparison because every B3 output was included, while each incumbent was preselected as its condition's Stage 1 leader.
- OpsRelay's best B3 output was 0.5 points behind the best incumbent. Stillroom's best B3 output tied for second in raw score and was the highest-ranked candidate that passed automated acceptance.
- B3 does not establish aesthetic superiority. It establishes that competitive visual quality and reliable acceptance can coexist when a render/repair loop is part of the workflow.
