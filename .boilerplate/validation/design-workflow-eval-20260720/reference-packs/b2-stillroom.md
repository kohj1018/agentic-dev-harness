## Task Tensions

- Expert credibility vs novice comprehension
- Tactile brand identity vs visible, familiar controls
- Rich product choice vs a short path to recurring price

## Designer Shortlist

### Onyx live configurator
- URL: https://onyxcoffeelab.com/pages/subscribe?product-id=4486024626274
- Role: shipped task/flow evidence
- Exact use: coffee -> amount -> schedule -> recurring-price sentence -> add to cart
- Borrow: plain-language summary of the selected recurring order
- Avoid: an excessive number of frequency choices without progressive disclosure

### Baymard consumables-subscription research
- URL: https://baymard.com/blog/new-research-consumables-subscription-services
- Role: behavior/UX evidence
- Exact use: clear overview, straightforward preference choice, widely visible pricing
- Borrow: disclose how it works and price at decision time
- Avoid: aesthetics inferred from research prose

### Monocle Shop
- URL: https://monocle.com/shop/
- Role: identity/craft target
- Borrow: concise editorial hierarchy and product confidence
- Avoid: importing magazine IA into the configurator

### Shopware Meteor
- URL: https://shopware.design/
- Role: one coherent primary implementation system
- Borrow: accessible commerce primitives and consistent selection states
- Avoid: mixing Radix/Polaris/Meteor token values into a composite palette

## Positive Identity Input

- Thesis: Make taste feel specific while keeping the purchase decision ordinary and trustworthy.
- Signature: A quiet tasting line connects flavor choice to the persistent order summary.
- Familiar convention preserved: segmented/radio selection -> review summary -> explicit subscribe action.
- Imagery: one controlled coffee image; no decorative stock collage.
- Density: medium on desktop, sequential and sticky-summary aware on mobile.
