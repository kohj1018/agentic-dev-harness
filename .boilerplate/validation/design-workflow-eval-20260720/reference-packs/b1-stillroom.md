## Designer Shortlist

### Onyx subscriptions
- URL: https://onyxcoffeelab.com/en-int/pages/subscriptions
- Role: direct task/aesthetic target
- Borrow: choose coffee -> frequency -> relax framing and confident product voice
- Avoid: long editorial copy inside the configurator

### Monocle Shop
- URL: https://monocle.com/shop/
- Role: adjacent editorial-commerce target
- Borrow: compact editorial labels and cultured product framing
- Avoid: magazine navigation complexity

### Awwwards coffee collection
- URL: https://www.awwwards.com/websites/coffee/
- Role: craft lead only
- Borrow: authored type/image relationships
- Avoid: motion spectacle, nominees treated as winners, and inaccessible novelty

### Shopware Meteor
- URL: https://shopware.design/
- Role: primary implementation system
- Borrow: coherent selection controls, accessibility, and commerce primitives
- Avoid: administration visual defaults

## Positive Identity Input

- Thesis: Turn coffee expertise into a tactile choice that never feels like a test.
- Signature: A tasting-line motif connects flavor, amount, cadence, and the order summary.
- Imagery: honest macro coffee photography with sharp editorial crops.
- Density: medium; enough comparison information without a long quiz.
- Concept range: one baseline-safe direction and one signature-forward direction.

## Caveat

The Awwwards and adjacent lanes improve breadth but do not establish subscription usability. The system source is implementation-groundable but not a consumer brand reference.
