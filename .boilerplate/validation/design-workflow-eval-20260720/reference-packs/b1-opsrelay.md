## Designer Shortlist

### incident.io
- URL: https://incident.io/
- Role: aesthetic/task target
- Borrow: human-centered incident language, one-place coordination
- Avoid: marketing hero and oversized promotional typography

### Oracle Trade Dashboard
- URL: https://docs.oracle.com/en/industries/financial-services/banking-trade-finance-service/14.8.0.0.0/tftds/trade-dashboard-user-guide.pdf
- Role: adjacent high-pressure operations
- Borrow: dense role-based queues and explicit work status
- Avoid: legacy form density and domain-specific banking chrome

### UX Design Awards yearbook
- URL: https://ux-design-awards.com/events-media/ux-design-awards-yearbook-2024/uxdesignawards_yearbook_2024.pdf
- Role: craft lead
- Borrow: intentional hierarchy and authored visual rhythm
- Avoid: treating award inclusion as usability or accessibility proof

### Grafana Saga
- URL: https://grafana.com/developers/saga
- Role: primary implementation system
- Borrow: coherent neutral/status primitives and dense product patterns
- Avoid: copying Grafana branding

## Positive Identity Input

- Thesis: Compress urgency into a calm, readable operating rhythm.
- Signature: A persistent incident-tempo rail that exposes state, age, and next handoff.
- Density: high, with deliberate breathing room around the selected incident.
- Concept range: one baseline-safe direction and one signature-forward direction.

## Caveat

The adjacent and award lanes produced weaker task evidence than the direct and system lanes. They remain in the pack because this condition tests breadth-forcing.
