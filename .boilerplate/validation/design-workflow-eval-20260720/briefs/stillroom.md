# Brief: Stillroom Coffee

## Product

A specialty coffee subscription configurator for curious home brewers.

## Persona and Situation

Jae makes pour-over coffee at home but is overwhelmed by origin and processing terminology. They want a recurring coffee that fits their taste without taking a long quiz.

## Primary Task

Choose a taste direction, bag amount, and delivery cadence, understand the recurring price and first shipment date, then subscribe.

## Required Surface

One usable subscription configurator containing:

- Three understandable taste directions
- Amount and cadence controls
- Persistent order summary with price and first shipment date
- Clear subscription action and cancellation reassurance
- Product imagery using the controlled image URL below

Controlled image URL:
`https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1447933601403-0c6688de566e?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1600&q=85`

## Constraints

- The actual configurator is the first screen, not a marketing landing page
- The coffee/product identity is visible in the first viewport
- Warm and tactile is allowed, but avoid a one-note beige/brown palette
- Mobile 375 must keep the price, selected options, and primary action understandable
- Real commerce copy; no feature-explainer prose about the interface
- No gradient text, glassmorphism, floating decorative cards, or generic three-card feature row
- Preserve familiar selection controls and a review-before-purchase summary

## Evaluation Task

Choose a bright, fruit-forward coffee, 2 bags every 2 weeks, and verify the recurring price before subscribing.
